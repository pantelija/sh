What types of straps does Wristbuddys sell?

Wristbuddys produces and sells two-piece rubber and leather straps.

Rubber Leather

Width: They offer straps for watches with 17mm, 18mm, 19mm, 20mm, 21mm, 22mm, or 24mm lug widths. The website is nicely organized as you can select a link to the corresponding strap size directly from their main navigation.

Material: Wristbuddys sells two-piece rubber and leather watch straps. Their rubber straps are made from a pliable and durable compound. In addition to standard straight-cut rubber straps, they also offer curved-end options.

For their leather collections customers can choose from cowhide, embossed alligator, Epsom, and suede

About Wristbuddys

Wristbuddys, based in Stockholm, Sweden, sets itself apart by offering affordable, high-quality watch straps and accessories, including custom-fit straps for popular models and brands. They have a massive inventory of rubber and leather straps designed or the popular brands and models (Tudor BB, Omega bioceramic watches, Tissot PRX, even for the MB&F Mad 1). They also sell tools and accessories like watch rolls, pouches, and cleaning products.

Samuel, the founder and passionate enthusiast, is working really hard to providing exceptional customer support and service. The company offers free express shipping on all orders, a lifetime warranty on rubber straps, and consistently receives positive customer reviews that praise product quality, fast delivery, and excellent service.

Deals, coupon codes, and promotions

They frequently offer promotions and discounts, typically ranging from 15% to 25%. Also, they have deals for multi-strap purchases. Customers often receive complimentary gifts, such as polishing cloths or spring bar tools.
