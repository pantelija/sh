What types of straps does ZEALANDE® sell?

They exclusively offer rubber twp-piece and pass-through watch straps in various colors and configurations.

Rubber Pass-through

Width: ZEALANDE® offers a broad range of lug widths to ensure compatibility with many luxury watch brands, including Omega, Tudor, Rolex, Breitling, Panerai and more. Their selection features both fitted curved-end straps and straight-end universal straps. The Universal Straps are available in a wide spectrum of sizes, from 18mm up to 24mm (specifically 18mm, 19mm, 20mm, 21mm, 22mm and 24mm).

Additionally, the company provides two different length options (Classic and Large) to ensure a perfect fit for a wide range of wrist sizes.

Material: ZEALANDE® makes straps from vulcanized FKM rubber, which they choose for exceptional durability, strength, and elasticity. This premium material is crafted by high-end manufacturers and is built to endure harsh conditions, offering resistance to wear, temperature changes, and chemicals. Their FKM rubber is treated to be anti-UV, anti-dust, and anti-allergic.

About ZEALANDE®

ZEALANDE® is a brand driven by the passion of its CEO, Alexandre P., a lifelong watch enthusiast and collector. After years spent engaging with the watch community, he officially took over the company in October 2018 with a singular mission: to establish ZEALANDE® as the benchmark for high-end rubber straps designed specifically for prestige watches.

The brand is built on the principle of premium quality, without compromise.

ZEALANDE® alos produces leather pouches, and precision assembly tools. The company's focus is defined in three main principles: Comfort, Craftsmanship, and Character.
