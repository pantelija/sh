Velcro and Hook & Loop watch straps

Single-pass or two-piece straps made of a durable material that fasten together with a hook-and-loop design. This makes it easy to get a perfect and secure fit.

Click to see the stores

Why we love them?

They are simple, effective, and provide the perfect fit every time. The hook-and-loop design creates a strong connection that you can easily micro-adjust for maximum comfort. Plus, they are super easy to put on and take off.

  • They are infinitely adjustable
  • They are super strong & durable
  • No break-in period required
  • They come in a range of colors and styles

In the photo: Omega Speedmaster on Haveston IVA Velcro.

What it the average price of a velcro and hook & loop strap?

These straps are generally considered to be an affordable option, and they can often be purchased for between $20 and $40.

Some higher-end straps might have a premium price, but they usually come from luxury brands and use exotic materials.
