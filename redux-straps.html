What types of straps does Redux Straps sell?

Redux Straps produces and offers pass-through straps in nylon, elastic, and leather, along with two-piece nylon straps.

Pass-through Elastic Leather

Width: Their straps come in two sizes, either 20mm or 22mm. Please keep in mind that not all collections have both sizes; some only come in 20mm.

Material: Redux uses high-quality materials. Their straps feature premium leathers, specifically calfskin and Horween Cordovan. Textile options include laser-cut ballistic nylon, elasticized nylon, and durable Hypalon rubber. All metal components, their buckles and keepers, are made from robust titanium.

About Redux Straps

Redux Straps is a small, family-run business, founded in 2012 by a husband and wife team. The name Redux, derived from Latin, means "brought back" or revived. They focus on creating iconic Everyday Carry (EDC) tools that encourage and equip courageous explorers to live with purpose and adventure.

The company was launched with a simple idea: to build a robust, reasonably sized timepiece inspired by WWII pilot watches, using modern materials. From 2015 to 2020, they prototyped their first success, the COURG timepiece, with input from over 2,300 allies. They later expanded their designs to include the HONNR, VALOR, and SCOUT series (2021-2024).

These watch names are Way Points—five-letter navigational tools pronounced as words of character, such as COURG (Courage). The business operates as a Collective (Co.) of diverse professionals and has engaged customers from over 70 nations globally.

Today, Redux has channeled their passion for robust, military-inspired design into their straps, which are built for extreme durability and feature titanium hardware. These rugged straps are a continuation of their commitment to high-performance materials and dependable gear for any mission.
