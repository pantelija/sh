What types of straps does CHROONOO sell?

Chronoo offers a great selection of watch straps in different configuration options, sizes, materials, and price ranges.

Leather Pass-through Sailcloth Perlon Elastic Canvas Metal Rubber

Width: They offer straps for watches with lug widths from 13mm up to 28mm. That's aweaome! CHROONOO online store is neatly organized as you can select a link to the corresponding size directly from their website's main navigation.

Material: CHROONOO offers a huge variety and great selection of strap materials. Their collection spans a wide range of leathers, including exotic and premium options like Alligator, Crocodile, Elephant, Lizard, Ostrich, Shark, Shell Cordovan, and Yak, alongside classic choices like Buffalo, Goat, Horse, Nubuck, and various types of Suede and Calfskin.

For non-leather performance and casual styles, they utilize durable fabrics like Nylon, Canvas, Cordura, Perlon, and Recycled Polyester.

Their technical and sport selections feature resilient rubber compounds such as FKM Rubber, PU, Rubber, and Silicone, with options also available in Vegan and Sustainable materials.

About CHROONOO

CHROONOO was founded by entrepreneurs Tilo Rupsch and Mick Jelsdal Jørgensen with the goal of becoming the premier online source for watch straps. They provide enthusiasts with a high-quality selection by sourcing products from trusted suppliers who prioritize craftsmanship and durability. Their catalog includes materials like leather, stainless steel, nylon, and rubber.

CHROONOO prioritizes customer satisfaction, offering dedicated support to assist with any inquiries and ensure a pleasant, hassle-free experience. As they continue to grow, they are constantly expanding their offerings and staying current with the latest trends and innovative designs in the watch industry.

They invite everyone, from collectors to casual wearers, to join them on their journey to become the premier online destination for watch straps.
