What types of straps does Quaint & Collectible sell?

Quaint & Collectible exclusively offers two-piece leather straps.

Leather Custom

Width: The straps are available in several standard lug width and taper combinations, including 16mm/16mm, 17mm/16mm, 18mm/16mm, 19mm/16mm, 20mm/16mm, and 20mm/18mm. These sizes cover a wide range of popular watches, but the company also offers a custom strap program for customers requiring other or specific dimensions.

Material: Quaint & Collectible sources only premium leather for their straps. Their selection includes high-quality options such as Alran Chevre, Togo, Epsom, Nubuck, and smooth calf. They also feature Buttero, alongside luxurious options like crocodile and alligator.

About Quaint & Collectible

Quaint & Collectible was founded by a dedicated watch enthusiast who, after years of collecting vintage and modern timepieces, struggled to find high-quality straps that were both well-made and affordably priced. Today, the company is a small operation run by the founder, who manages communications and planning, and their partner, who meticulously crafts each strap by hand.

Quaint & Collectible is committed to 100% handcrafting in small batches, a process that begins with the careful selection of the finest full-grain, genuine leather. Each hide is hand-selected in their workshop.

After selection, the leather is precisely cut and then crafted into a watch strap by hand, a small-batch approach that results in the superior quality.
