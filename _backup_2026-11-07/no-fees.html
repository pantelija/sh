Watch strap stores that ship with no import fees for US buyers

Stores outside the US that ship duties paid, so you pay nothing extra when your strap arrives.



👌 12 stores in the list

Since the US ended the duty-free de minimis exemption in August 2025, many orders from overseas arrive with surprise tariffs, customs duties and courier handling fees. The stores below prepay all duties and taxes on US orders (Delivered Duty Paid), so the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Always check the shipping option you pick at checkout, as some stores offer duties-paid delivery only with selected services. Looking for shops based in the United States? See our list of US watch strap stores.
