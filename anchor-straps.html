What types of straps does Anchor Straps sell?

Anchor Straps offers a great selection of two-piece straps with deployant or standard buckles, as well as pass-through straps in various configurations and materials. They also feature stainless steel bracelets.

Leather Pass-through Sailcloth Elastic Metal Rubber

Width: Their most popular models are made in widths of 18mm, 20mm, and 22mm. If you need a strap for watches with 21mm lug width, then you can find several options in their deploynat sailcloth or ribbed nylon collections.

Material: Their core offerings include FKM Rubber (used in AnchorFlex and Single-Pass lines, including straps for Omega and Tudor), various types of Nylon (such as Deployant, Ribbed, Hybrid, and Ranger Velcro styles), and classic options like Pueblo leather and Sailcloth. They also offer steel bracelets.

About Anchor Straps Co.

Built by watch enthusiasts and made for the watch community, Anchor Strap Co. is a small, family-run business based in Washington State. Their journey began with a simple belief: a great watch strap should enhance a timepiece and be ready for any adventure.

They design and test every product, from sailcloth and FKM rubber to premium leather and nylon, with a focus on durability, comfort, and design. Every strap is a result of real-world testing and feedback from the community they are proud to be a part of.

Their passion project started in 2022 by modifying watches and hunting for better straps. This led to the creation of the first prototype of their signature Deployant Nylon Strap in 2023. Since launching their first products in early 2024, they have continued to expand their materials and introduce new lines all while staying true to their roots as a family business.
