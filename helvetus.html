What types of straps does Helvetus® sell?

Helvetus® offers the highest-quality two-piece leather and rubber straps, metal bracelets, nylon pass-through straps, as well as made-to-order custom straps.

Rubber Leather Metal Pass-through Custom

Width: They offer curved-end rubber straps, universal straight-end rubber straps, CTS (cut-to-size) rubber straps, leather straps, and bracelets designed for the most popular watches from leading brands. This means their straps perfectly match your watch's lug width.

In addition, they sell universal straps in widths ranging from 12mm to 24mm. Some collections, such as alligator, feature all these dimensions, others, like ostrich, offer fewer options. Please check each collection to see the available widths.

Material: They craft their straps from both rubber and leather. The company also sells replacement nylon straps and stainless steel bracelets.

Helvetus® rubber straps are made from either vulcanized or FKM rubber. Vulcanization is a process that improves the rubber's properties, increasing strength, flexibility, and resistance to heat.

For leather, their selection includes calfskin as well as exotic options like alligator and ostrich.

About Helvetus®

Helvetus® began as the shared vision of three young students who were deeply immersed in the luxury watch market. Starting from humble beginnings in local craft markets with a few trusted suppliers, the brand has grown into Switzerland's premier store and a global leader in the aftermarket watch strap industry.

This growth is built on a foundation of strong customer relationships and a customer-centric approach, where customer feedback and needs directly inspire and shape all collections.

The brand's core philosophy is a commitment to excellence and quality. Helvetus® offers over 86 distinct strap styles designed to complement more than 16 watch brands.

Helvetus® is strategically positioned in Lucerne, the heart of Switzerland's luxury watch industry. This proximity allows everyone in the team to stay intimately connected with the latest trends and innovations. This helps them create products that reflect the Swiss heritage of fine craftsmanship, precision, and luxury.
