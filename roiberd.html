Leather Custom

Width: Roiberd Atelier offers ready-made straps in popular sizes like 20mm/16mm and 20/18mm, but they also do custom work. While all variations from 16mm to 24mm are possible to order, you can submit a special request for specific needs, and they will likely be able to manufacture a strap in your requested dimensions.

Material: All their straps are made from the highest quality leather, sourced from renowned tanneries. The list of leather is extensive: French Chèvre, Babele, Goatskin, Epsom, Dollaro, Buttero, Pueblo, and Saffiano. They also use exotics such as an alligator leather.

About Roiberd Atelier

Every leather strap and accessory from Roiberd Atelier is a testament to the passion and great attention to detail of its owner, Roberto. What began as a personal passion for handcrafted goods evolved into a business dedicated to the art of leatherworking.

Based in his Spanish atelier, Roberto makes sure that each piece is crafted with the same level of care and precision, from custom orders to ready-made collections. He uses only top-quality leather sourced from the finest tanneries. Every strap is hand-stitched with waxed linen thread and finished with refined artisanal techniques.

Roberto is proud of his ability to craft a strap that perfectly complements both the timepiece and customer's personal style. Whether a client is looking for a refined strap for a dress watch, or a robust option for daily use, Roiberd Atelier always delivers the right product.
