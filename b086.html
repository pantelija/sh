NDC Straps Featured in a Major Hollywood Film

We've written about NDC Straps before, and we'll happily admit that we're fans. So when this story crossed our path, we had to share it. We stumbled on it over on Watch Brothers' Instagram and sat down to give it a proper read. It's not often that something like this happens to a small, enthusiast-focused strap maker, and we at StrapHunter wanted to give them a shoutout.

Nenad Pantelic • July 7, 2026

Here's the short version. A worn NDC strap ended up in an upcoming Hollywood survival thriller directed by David Ayer and starring Brad Pitt. There was no sponsorship, no placement deal, no marketing arrangement of any kind. It simply happened, and it was confirmed to the maker only after filming had wrapped.

How the production came to source that particular strap, and the watch it was paired with on screen, is where the story gets good, and it's not really ours to tell.

Credit where it's due: Watch Brothers did an excellent job connecting the dots and turning it into a proper piece for their site. So rather than repeat it here, we'll point you to them.

Read the full story "How NDC Straps Landed a Role in Brad Pitt's New Film" on the Watch Brothers website.

And, of course, you can check out NDC Straps to see their full lineup of period-correct parachute elastic bands.
