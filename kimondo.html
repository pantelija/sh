About Kimondo and Co.

Kimondo & Co. is a retailer specializing in watch straps, accessories, and timepieces. They offer a diverse catalog built from quality materials, including Italian and German leather, suede, Cordura, and japanese vegan leather.

The company organizes straps by distinctive style series and offers products tailored for specific brands like Rolex and Omega. Beyond straps, Kimondo & Co. sells accessories such as premium Cuban humidors and watch stands.
