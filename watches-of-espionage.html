About Watches Of Espionage

Watches of Espionage (W.O.E.) is a brand and community run by a former Case Officer with the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) who maintains a deep passion for timepieces. The company is rooted in the founder's experience, where a reliable watch was essential regardless of the mission or location. W.O.E. also honors the men and women who served.

W.O.E. offers a selection of watch straps within their Shop section. They emphasize quality and heritage, partnering with suppliers like Leather Works Minnesota, known for making leather goods by hand in the United States, and Zulu Alpha Straps from the UK.

Their retail focus complements the W.O.E. community, which explores the role of watches through the lens of special operations, intelligence, and culture.
