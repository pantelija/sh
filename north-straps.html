What types of straps does North Straps sell?

North Straps offers a diverse selection that includes two-piece straps (fitted with standard or deployant buckles) and pass-through straps in various configurations and materials.

Leather Pass-through Sailcloth Rubber

Width: North Straps offers straps in the three most common lug widths: 18mm, 20mm, and 22mm. The straps typically feature either a 2mm taper (for example 20mm/18mm) or a 4mm taper (for example 22mm/18mm).

Material: For Leather, they offer premium hides including Buttero, Babele, Barenia, Epsom, Horween Chromexcel, and Pueblo.

Their Rubber performance straps are made from durable FKM Rubber in various styles (Foldover, Tropical, Kraken, Waffle), and they also offer Cut to Size (CTS) and Crosshatch rubber.

The Military category includes robust textile options like Ribbed, Premium, and Tough military straps.

Finally, their Sailcloth category offers resilient designs like the Fathoms Sailcloth Classic and Fathoms Sailcloth Foldover.
