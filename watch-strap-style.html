What types of straps does Watch Strap Style sell?

Width: Watch Strap Style offers straps in an exceptionally wide range of lug widths to fit virtually any timepiece, from the smallest vintage watch to the largest modern design. Their sizes range from as narrow as 6mm up to 40mm, specifically covering 6mm, 8mm, 10mm, 12mm, 14mm, 16mm, 18mm, 20mm, 22mm, 24mm, 26mm, 28mm, 30mm, 32mm, 34mm, 36mm, 38mm, and 40mm.

Additionally, customers can choose between two length options: Standard (less than 190mm and Extra Long (more than 190mm).

Material: Their collection includes classic materials like leather, suede, nylon, rubber, silicone, fabric, and tweed.

For exotic options, they feature genuine alligator, genuine crocodile, genuine lizard, and genuine python.

They also cater to specialized requirements with materials such as Cordura, microfibre, anti allergy, and vegan leather.

About Watch Strap Style

Watch Strap Style was founded by passionate watch enthusiasts in Lancashire who aimed to create the largest online collection of replacement watch straps. Founder Martyn Bleasdale says that their passion for horology maintains that "a timepiece is nothing without the right strap. It can literally transform a watch from a mere timekeeping device into a full-on style statement and expression of your personality."

The company's mission is to be a trusted place for watch owners, operating with honesty, integrity, and fair pricing. The collection is extensive and curated, compatible with major brands from Seiko, Apple Watch to Tag Heuer. Watch Strap Style is committed to continuously updating its selection and ensuring a high-quality customer experience.
